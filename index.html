About Us

Born in the small Canadian beach town of White Rock, The Ticket Takers are a four-piece band that brings high energy to catchy melodies and angular riffs.

Comprised of Hayden Althuizen (guitar/vocals), Walter Dingwall (vocals/guitar), Keaton Althuizen (bass), and Dallas Spani (drums), the group formed in November 2023.
